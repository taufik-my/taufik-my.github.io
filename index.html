Hi World! I am Taufik 🙋

📝 EPA @ TU Delft

🎓 Previously CS, BTM, STP @ KAIST

🌱 Justus & Louise van Effen Scholar

I am a Master’s student in Engineering and Policy Analysis at TU Delft in the Netherlands. I am honored to be a recipient of the Justus & Louise van Effen Excellence Scholarship .

I graduated from KAIST, where I double majored in Computer Science and Business and Technology Management, with a minor in Science and Technology Policy. During my undergraduate studies, I received the Global Korea Scholarship (2020), the Daewoong Global Scholarship Program (2022 and 2023), and the Lee&Won Asian Fellowship (2023).

I am interested in applying technology, especially AI, for social good, with a focus on computational sustainability, technology policy, and systems thinking.

Outside of academics, I love traveling 🗺️ and photography 📸, and I enjoy singing in my free time 🎶. I am also fascinated by startup culture and policy-making. At the end of the day, I just hope my work can contribute something meaningful to the world. Welcome to my personal website! 😬
